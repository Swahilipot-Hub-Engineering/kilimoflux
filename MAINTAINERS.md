# Maintainers

Who looks after this project, and what you can expect from us.

## Project team

| Role | Person | Responsibilities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Maintainer** | [@jkose002](https://github.com/jkose002) | Roadmap, final review, releases, repository settings |
| **Mentor** | [@Swahilipot-Hub-Engineering/mentors](https://github.com/orgs/Swahilipot-Hub-Engineering/teams/mentors) | Onboarding new contributors, answering questions, `good first issue` support |
| **Reviewers** | [@Swahilipot-Hub-Engineering/reviewers](https://github.com/orgs/Swahilipot-Hub-Engineering/teams/reviewers) | Code review within 2 working days |
| **Design** | @<handle> | UI, UX, accessibility |

Review routing is automated through [`.github/CODEOWNERS`](.github/CODEOWNERS).

## What you can expect from us

| Situation | Our commitment |
| --- | --- |
| You open your first issue or comment | First response within **2 working days** |
| You ask to be assigned an issue | Assigned within **2 working days** |
| You open a pull request | First review within **2 working days** |
| You are stuck and ask for help | We help — no judgement, no "you should already know this" |
| We cannot get to it | We say so, and give you a realistic timeframe |

If you have waited longer than this, that is our failure, not yours. Comment again and tag
`@Swahilipot-Hub-Engineering/maintainers`.

## What we ask of you

- Claim one issue at a time, and comment if you go quiet for a while.
- Keep pull requests focused and reasonably small.
- Respond to review comments, or say if you need to step away.
- Follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Maintainer duties

Maintainers are expected to:

- Triage new issues twice a week (add `type:`, `priority:`, `area:`, `level:`).
- **Keep at least 5 open `good first issue`s available at all times.** When the funnel is
  empty, new contributors have nowhere to land.
- Review PRs within 2 working days, or hand over to another reviewer.
- Keep `README.md`, `AGENTS.md`, and `.github/copilot-instructions.md` accurate — stale
  instructions actively mislead both people and AI agents.
- Update the project's Delivery board and portfolio Health field monthly.
- Nominate consistent contributors for trusted-contributor and reviewer status.

## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainers are nominated by existing maintainers after sustained, high-quality
contribution — typically several months of reviewing others' work as well as your own. See
the [contributor ladder](https://github.com/Swahilipot-Hub-Engineering/handbook/blob/main/03-contributor-journey.md#37-grow--the-contributor-ladder).

## Stepping down

Stepping back is normal and completely fine. Open an issue or tell the Hub team so we can
hand over cleanly, and update this file. Thank you for the time you gave.
